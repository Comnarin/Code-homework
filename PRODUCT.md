# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Customers of a small food store, using their own phone while choosing an order, often standing in a queue or sitting at a table. They want to know what their order will cost, including discounts, before they pay. Sessions are short: pick quantities, optionally enter a member card, read the total.

## Product Purpose

A price calculator for a food store with seven set menus (Red, Green, Blue, Yellow, Pink, Purple, Orange). It shows the price before discount, which items got a pair discount, the member-card discount and the final total. Success is a customer who understands exactly why the final price is what it is, in a few taps, without asking staff.

## Brand Personality

Clean, premium, calm. Confident typography and generous space, like a modern cafe or payments app. The seven colour-named sets are the only strong colour in the interface; everything else stays quiet.

## Anti-references

- The generic SaaS dashboard: default blue buttons, white cards on grey, grey body text.
- Cluttered screens with many borders, shadows or decoration.

## Design Principles

1. **The price is the hero.** Totals and discounts get the clearest hierarchy; everything else supports reading them.
2. **Show the arithmetic.** Every discount is a visible line, so the final total can be checked by eye.
3. **Thumb first.** Controls are large and reachable one-handed; the primary action is always in reach.
4. **Colour carries meaning, not decoration.** The set colours identify products; the interface itself stays restrained.
5. **Calm under error.** Problems (a held Red set, a bad card, no network) are explained plainly and never lose the customer's order.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: 4.5:1 text contrast (3:1 for large text), visible focus states, touch targets at least 44px, no information conveyed by colour alone (every colour-named set also shows its name), and `prefers-reduced-motion` respected for all motion.
